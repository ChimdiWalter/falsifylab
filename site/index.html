FalsifyLab — Where Scientific Laws Meet Counterexamples.

A law can fit every observation you have and still fail outside the conditions where it was measured. FalsifyLab spends a counted experiment budget trying to break a candidate law, revises it only within a declared family, freezes the revision, and lets independent measurements decide. Every reading is kept as evidence.

Interactive counterexample discovery · nonlinear dynamics

An apparent conservation law meets a counterexample.

Short, low-amplitude trajectories make a quadratic energy Q = v² + a·x² look conserved. A fixed 15-condition stress catalog tests it at larger amplitudes and longer horizons. If it is refuted, a quartic term is allowed, the model is frozen, and three new trajectories decide.

How to read the controls

Duffing adds a cubic restoring force. Its true invariant is v² + ω²x² + (α/2)x⁴, so a quadratic is refuted at large amplitude.

Harmonic control has a truly quadratic invariant; the correct outcome is not refuted (or abstention under noise).

Damped control loses energy at dQ/dt = −2γv²; no polynomial invariant exists, so the correct outcome is that the revision is also refuted.

Evidence records

Inspect what was actually recorded.

Each run exports a JSON evidence record (schema 1.0.0) with partitions, the model-freeze fingerprint, every experiment and its cost, raw-data hashes and limitations. python -m falsifylab.verify outputs recomputes the metrics from the raw arrays. A record shows internal consistency and reproducibility. It is not a mathematical proof.
